Why This Track?

Many Digital Twin initiatives struggle because practitioners cannot clearly answer a few fundamental questions:

What are we twinning?
How will it work?
Why are we building it?
What value will it create?
Who needs to be involved?

Without clear answers, projects suffer from misaligned expectations, unclear scope, and difficulty demonstrating value.

This track provides a practical framework to help practitioners bring greater clarity, alignment, and confidence to Digital Twin initiatives.

What You'll Build

By the end of the track, you will have a complete Digital Twin planning package consisting of:

Digital Twin Readiness Checklist
Communication Diagnostic Toolkit

These outputs can be adapted and reused within your own organization and projects.
