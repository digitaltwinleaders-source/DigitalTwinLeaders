Digital Twin Project Planner

Plan a Digital Twin in 6 Steps

The Digital Twin Project Planner (DTPP) is a self-paced, vendor-neutral track that takes you from "is this really a digital twin?" to a complete project canvas in six chapters. It is inspired by ISO/IEC 30173.

Each chapter ends with a planning tool you keep, so by the end you have a full planning package ready for stakeholder discussions, project definition and the business case.
