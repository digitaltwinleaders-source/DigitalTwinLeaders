Who We Serve

Digital Twin Leaders brings together practitioners already working on digital twins and professionals moving into the field from BIM, GIS, engineering, and related roles, from experts and strategists to academics and early-career professionals.

Teams, institutions, and partners join too, helping their people learn from peers and share knowledge across the field. Together, members advance the responsible and impactful use of digital twins worldwide.
