WHAT DTL IS

Digital Twin Leaders is a vendor-neutral global community for people working on digital twins. We keep the same distance from every platform and are tied to none.

Members publish what they learn, speak about it, are listed publicly as practitioners, and plan projects with a method inspired by ISO/IEC 30173.

RECOGNITION

Practitioner Status is earned by completing the Planner. It comes with a certificate of completion, a badge on your listing, and a LinkedIn post introducing your work. It recognises completion of a DTL track and is not an accredited qualification.

Seniority follows your membership years: Member in year one, Senior Member in year two, Lead Practitioner in year three, Fellow from year four. Levels advance at renewal; if a membership lapses, it starts again at Member.
