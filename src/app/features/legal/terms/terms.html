DTL Membership Terms

Last updated: 22 November 2026

These terms apply to membership of Digital Twin Leaders (DTL), run by Digital Twin Leaders LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. By joining, you agree to them.

1. Membership and price

  • Council Membership: $299 per year, a founding rate for the first 50 members, which you keep while your membership continues without a break.
  • Team Membership: $699 per year for 3 named people, plus $199 for each extra person. After purchase, we'll ask you for your team members' details by email.
  • Partner or invited members: a free first year with the same benefits as paying members. If you joined through our online checkout, it renews at $299 per year unless you cancel before the renewal date. If DTL added you directly, it ends after one year unless you choose to continue at $299 per year. Either way, we email you before the free year ends.
  • Prices are set in US dollars and may be shown as a monthly equivalent, but are billed annually in advance. Checkout may show your local currency, converted at the rate displayed at checkout. Payments are processed by Stripe.

2. Renewal and cancellation

  • Paid memberships renew automatically every year at the same rate until you cancel. We email you before each renewal.
  • You can cancel at any time by emailing community@digitaltwinleaders.com or through the manage-subscription link in your receipt and renewal emails. If you cancel, your membership stays active until the end of your current term, including during a free first year. For refund eligibility and how to request a refund, see our Refund Policy.
  • If a renewal payment fails and your payment details aren't updated, your membership ends.
  • If your membership ends, DTL may remove your public member listing or keep it visible with the label "Former member." You can ask us to remove your listing at any time by emailing community@digitaltwinleaders.com.
  • If you rejoin after a break, your seniority level starts again at Member. Your Practitioner Status and certificate stay valid.

3. What membership includes

  • From day one: the Council Member title, your listing on the DTL website, and access to the Digital Twin Project Planner in the DTL Knowledge Hub.
  • Once your profile is complete: a welcome post on DTL's LinkedIn page.
  • Seniority: your level moves up one step with each renewal (Member, Senior Member, Lead Practitioner, Fellow), shown on your member badge.
  • Practitioner Status: earned by completing the Digital Twin Project Planner. It comes with a badge, a certificate of completion and a congratulations post on DTL's LinkedIn page. It recognises completion of a DTL track and is not an accredited qualification.

4. Publishing and speaking

These are opportunities, not guarantees.

  • From day one, all members can submit articles, request a written interview and apply to speak at DTL events.
  • DTL reviews and edits every piece and may decline it. You approve the final version before it is published.
  • Accepted pieces follow DTL's content calendar. DTL does not guarantee a publication date or a speaking slot, and may set fair limits on how often each member publishes.
  • You keep ownership of your work and give DTL a non-exclusive right to publish and share it on DTL channels, including after your membership ends. This right ends for content published during a term that is refunded.
  • Pieces must be vendor-neutral and based on your own experience, and you must have permission to share any client, project or employer information.

5. Conduct

DTL may end a membership, without refund, if a member misuses the member directory, uses DTL channels for sales outreach, misrepresents DTL, or shares other members' information without consent.

6. Changes

DTL may change membership benefits. Changes apply to current members from their next renewal, and we tell you by email first. A change never removes your Practitioner Status, your certificate or the seniority level you currently hold.

7. Liability

DTL content is shared for professional learning, not as professional advice for a specific project. DTL's total liability to a member is limited to the fees that member paid in the previous 12 months.
