Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Digital Twin Leaders LLC (DTL), 30 N Gould St, Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA, collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Membership details: name, email, role, company, country, photo, LinkedIn profile link and short bio.
  • Learning progress and work you submit in the DTL Knowledge Hub.
  • Email subscription details, if you sign up for DTL updates.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details.

How we use it

To run your membership, show your member listing, give you Knowledge Hub access, review your submitted work, publish your contributions, process payments and send you DTL emails.

Legal basis (EU and UK)

We use your information to provide the membership you joined (contract), to send updates you asked for (consent, which you can withdraw by unsubscribing), to keep records the law requires such as tax records (legal obligation), and to keep DTL secure and working (legitimate interests).

What is public

Your member listing (name, photo, role, company, country, bio, LinkedIn link), DTL's LinkedIn welcome and congratulations posts about you, and anything you publish with DTL are public. You decide what goes in your listing.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run DTL. We do not sell your information.

Cookies

Our website, Knowledge Hub and checkout use only the cookies needed to work, such as login and secure payment. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

While you are a member or subscriber. If your membership ends, DTL may remove your public member listing or keep it visible with the label "Former member." You can ask us to remove your listing at any time by emailing community@digitaltwinleaders.com. Contributions you published with DTL may stay available, as set out in our Terms. We keep payment records for as long as tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your other information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and you can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in each email. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).
