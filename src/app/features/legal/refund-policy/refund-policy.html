Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Not the right fit? You can get a full refund within 14 days, without giving a reason, as long as the conditions below are met.

When you can get a refund

  • Within 14 days of your payment.
  • For your first payment, as long as you have completed no more than two chapters of the Digital Twin Project Planner.
  • Automatic renewals: if you didn't mean to renew, ask within 14 days of the renewal payment. The chapter limit does not apply to automatic renewal payments, including the first automatic charge after a free year.
  • For a Team Membership's first payment (not a renewal), no team member may have completed more than two chapters of the Digital Twin Project Planner. Any refund covers the full team payment.

How to ask

Email community@digitaltwinleaders.com from the email address you joined with. We process refunds within 5 business days, back to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

After a refund

  • First payment refunded: your membership ends, and your listing, your Knowledge Hub access and anything published for you on DTL channels are removed.
  • Renewal refunded (including the first automatic charge after a free year, even if it is your first payment): your membership ends and your Knowledge Hub access is removed. Anything published during the refunded term is removed. Content published during your earlier membership terms, including a free year, stays, and your listing follows the "Former member" rule in our Terms.

After 14 days

Payments are not refundable after 14 days. You can cancel at any time to stop your next renewal; your membership stays active until the end of your current term.
